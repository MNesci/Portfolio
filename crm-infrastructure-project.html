CRM & GTM Operations Infrastructure

Building scalable CRM systems through automation, data architecture, and operational workflows

Challenge

As marketing and sales processes became more complex, the HubSpot CRM needed to support increasingly sophisticated campaigns, lead management, sales processes, and reporting requirements. New business needs often required additional data, automation and reporting capabilities, making it important to build systems that could adapt without increasing manual administrative work.

Approach

My focus was on building a CRM foundation that could evolve alongside the business. Rather than solving each operational request independently, I looked for opportunities to create reusable systems that improved data consistency, reduced manual effort, and supported future GTM initiatives.

Whenever Marketing or Sales identified a new operational requirement, I evaluated how it could be incorporated into the CRM through custom properties, workflows, automation, or reporting rather than introducing additional manual processes. I treated the CRM as shared operational infrastructure, designing solutions that could be standardized and reused across campaigns, lead management processes, and Sales workflows.

Key Decisions

  • Designed and managed custom HubSpot properties to support segmentation, reporting and automation
  • Built automation workflows to manage contact properties, lifecycle data, segmentation and outreach processes
  • Created workflows to maintain object associations and improve data integrity of across contacts, companies and deals
  • Developed automated notifications and follow-up tasks based on key prospect activity, enabling Sales to respond quickly to high-value engagement
  • Created new CRM properties and supporting workflows during a Pipedrive migration to preserve historical CRM data and maintain reporting continuity
  • Exported and analyzed CRM data to support reporting, operational decisions, and ongoing process improvements

Results

  • Reduced manual administration work through automation and standardized CRM processes
  • Improved data consistency and relationship integrity across contacts, companies and deals
  • Enabled faster Sales follow-up through automated notifications and task management
  • Maintained reporting continuity through the Pipedrive-to-HubSpot migration
  • Created reusable systems that reduced the time and effort required to support new campaigns and operational requirements
  • Improved visibility into CRM and marketing performance through standardized reporting and data management
