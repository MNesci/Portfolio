The problem

Third-party interfaces were slowing down client service

Ensuring proper reimbursement for radiology services demands specialized knowledge. Dexios helps hospitals, radiology groups, and imaging centers save time and money through accurate billing and regulatory compliance.

Before Iguana, Dexios depended on an external third-party integration service to manage interfaces between client systems and the ImagineSoftware billing platform for claims processing. Without direct control, the team encountered four persistent challenges.

01

High deployment costs

Each new interface could cost as much as $7,000.

02

Significant wait times

New interfaces could take up to 90 days, with minor updates delayed by three to four weeks.

03

Complex onboarding

Acting as an intermediary between clients and the external integration service added unnecessary complications.

04

Limited troubleshooting visibility

A lack of searchable interface logs made it harder to investigate and resolve data issues.

These bottlenecks prompted Dexios to look for an in-house solution that would restore direct control over integration processes.

The solution

Bringing radiology integrations in-house with Iguana

Dexios needed an integration engine that combined control with the flexibility to connect a diverse range of radiology systems. Iguana offered a balance of cost-efficiency, a user-friendly interface, and comprehensive documentation.

The iNTERFACEWARE team supported the transition through on-site training, integration expertise, ongoing education, and support. This prepared Dexios to manage its interfaces independently.

Iguana in action: streamlined workflows for radiology claims processing

Dexios now connects with hospitals, imaging centers, and Radiology Information Systems (RIS) including Powerscribe, EPIC, Medinformatix, Fuji, Cerner, CPSI, eRAD, and Ensemble. Interfaces capture demographics and results messages from these systems, then Iguana transforms the data into standardized HL7 messages for the ImagineSoftware billing system.
